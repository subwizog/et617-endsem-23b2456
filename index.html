About This Application ET 617

Built for ET 617 End-Semester Examination.

Candidate Roll No: 23B2456

Problem: P5 Inquiry-based virtual laboratory

Topic: T10 Projectile motion (angle & speed vs range)

Learner: L6 Olympiad learner (Ananya, 16, Class 11)

Design Constraint: C3 Mobile-first, one-handed (360 px width)

Physics Model & Assumptions
  • Ideal projectile motion: no air resistance.
  • Gravitational acceleration g = 9.8 m/s² (uniform, downward).
  • Flat landing surface at y = 0. Launch height h ≥ 0.
  • Ranges R are measured horizontally from launch x = 0.
C3: One-Handed Mobile Design

Optimized for 360 px screen width. All key actions, steppers, and navigation sit in the bottom thumb zone with tap targets ≥ 48 px. No drag-and-drop or hover-only interactions.
